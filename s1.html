OUR VISION

To build India's most advanced hybrid renewable-energy ecosystem by integrating solar, wind, and biogas with sustainable agriculture—empowering rural communities, strengthening energy security, and driving long-term environmental and economic prosperity.

MISSION STATEMENT

World-Class Energy Assets

Develop and operate 150 MW Solar, 80 MW Wind, and 20 TPD Biogas plants with cutting-edge technology.

Agrivoltaics & Biomass

Promote dual land use through agrivoltaics and biomass cultivation, maximizing productivity while generating clean energy.

Farmer Buy-Back Programme

Establish assured income for farmers through Napier grass and biomass buy-back, ensuring sustainable feedstock supply.

Rural Employment

Generate large-scale rural employment, skill development, and foster inclusive community growth across regions.

Green Technology

Adopt innovative green technologies, digital automation, and efficient waste-to-energy solutions for optimal performance.

National Impact

Contribute to India's clean-energy transition, carbon reduction targets, and sustainable development goals.

STRATEGIC GOALS

Six pillars driving our renewable energy revolution

1
Green Energy Generation
  • Commission 150 MW solar, 80 MW wind, and 20 TPD biogas facility
  • Ensure reliable, efficient, and environmentally responsible power supply
2
Sustainable Agriculture & Biomass
  • Cultivate Napier grass and energy crops on project and contract-farming land
  • Implement Buy-Back Assurance Scheme for guaranteed biomass procurement
  • Provide farmers with superior seedlings, training, and technical support
3
Rural Development & Social Impact
  • Create 1,000+ direct and 2,500+ indirect jobs
  • Increase farmer income through biomass purchase agreements
  • Promote rural entrepreneurship and community development
4
Technology & Innovation
  • Deploy automation, AI-based monitoring, and smart-grid integration
  • Maintain high performance ratios and >95% operational uptime
5
Economic & Financial Growth
  • Build scalable model with strong IRR, NPV, and DSCR
  • Expand hybrid energy and biomass model to multiple states
6
Environmental Stewardship
  • Reduce carbon emissions through integrated renewable solutions
  • Promote soil health, water conservation, and responsible land management
